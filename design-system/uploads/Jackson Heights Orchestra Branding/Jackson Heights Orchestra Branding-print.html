02 / Concept
The Idea

An orchestra as many voices — tuned to the rhythm of Jackson Heights.

01

Neighborhood, not institution

Warm, civic, human. The brand feels like a block in Queens — not a concert hall lobby. Serif display + honest sans.

02

A two-color spine, a mosaic chorus

Subway blue and Tandoor red lead every layout. Cilantro and Saffron join on dark grounds. The rest of the palette appears only as a thin rainbow rule — the mosaic of the neighborhood.

03

One mark, single colour

The wordmark sets in one colour at a time — Subway or Tandoor on paper, Cilantro or Saffron on ink, Black or White as needed. Never multi-toned.

04

Built for Squarespace

Everything designed in blocks a volunteer can rearrange — sections, color swaps, Google Fonts, nothing exotic.

03 / Logo System

Logo directions.

Three marks, one system

The Wordmark

Three stacked words in a single brand colour. The full palette stays out of the logo — it shows up underneath, as a rainbow rule, so the mark always reads clean.

Diversity as chord

Seven colors named for the neighborhood: the spices on 74th, the subway line overhead, the jacarandas in spring, the hibiscus on a fruit cart. The palette is a chorus, not a stack — it only appears together as a mosaic rule or grid, never inside the wordmark itself.

Rules of use

Wordmark. Always one color — Subway, Tandoor, Cilantro, Saffron, Ink, or Paper. Never multi-toned.
Primary. Subway and Tandoor lead every layout — wordmark colour, headlines, paper grounds, the dominant accent.
Highlights. Cilantro and Saffron carry the brand on dark or black backgrounds, and pop as callout colour on light.
Accents. Jacaranda, Hibiscus, Roosevelt only inside the full mosaic rule or accent grid — never alone.
Neutral. Newsprint is paper; Ink (#141A2A) is text. No pure black, no pure white.

12 / Handoff

The system, at a glance.

What you ship on day one
01

Logo.

Primary stacked wordmark, monogram tile (JHO), secondary waveform mark. SVG + PNG + single-color EPS.

02

Palette.

Seven named colors: Tandoor, Saffron, Cilantro, Subway, Jacaranda, Hibiscus, Roosevelt. Hex + CMYK + Pantone near-match.

03

Type.

Playfair Display for headlines, Inter for body. Both free on Google Fonts — available directly in Squarespace.

04

Posters.

Three 8.5 × 11 layouts (Big Type, Color Block, Editorial). Canva + InDesign templates for monthly swaps.

05

Instagram.

Square concert + brand templates · three story variants · caption voice guide.

06

Website.

Squarespace home + concerts page · native blocks · one-click color swap via CSS variables in Design panel.

07

Griffin.

Kept as a heritage seal in the footer — a quiet nod to the current identity, redrawn as a simple mark.

08

Voice.

Warm, direct, second person. Literal titles, honest copy. No exclamation points except in genuine welcomes.

What happens next.

  • Approve logo direction + final palette (open in Tweaks →).
  • Order business cards, letterhead, and program covers.
  • Migrate jhorchestra.org to Squarespace using this layout.

Try the Tweaks panel.

  • Swap palettes: Mosaic · Classical · Civic · Dusk.
  • Toggle the griffin heritage seal on/off.
  • Cycle poster layouts A / B / C from slide 07.
