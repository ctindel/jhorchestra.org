The heritage griffin seal — fine black line art of a winged griffin over the Queens skyline, inside a circular rule. It is a *seal*, not the logo: it sits quietly in poster and program footers beside the wordmark, never in a masthead and never as a substitute for the wordmark.

```jsx
<GriffinSeal size={68} base="../../" />
```

Because it is dense line art it stops reading below **56px** — that is a hard floor, not a suggestion (at 44px it collapses into a blob). Footers use 56–88px; a program cover can go to 140px. Never place it on a busy or mid-tone ground, and **never reverse it** — inverted, the fine line art breaks apart and reads as noise. On an Ink ground, sit the seal on a Paper or Newsprint chip instead.
