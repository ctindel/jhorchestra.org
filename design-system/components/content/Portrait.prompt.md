Portrait block used on posters, IG tiles and story headshots. Omitting `src` falls back to the Subway→Roosevelt gradient tile — never a stock photo.

```jsx
<Portrait src="../../assets/photos/alex-wen.jpg" name="Alex Wen" role="Guest Conductor" />
<Portrait src="…/michael-lee.jpg" name="Michael A. Lee" role="Composer" shape="round" size={88} />
<Portrait src="…/alex-wen.jpg" name="Alex Wen" role="Artistic Director" size={190} layout="stacked" />
```
