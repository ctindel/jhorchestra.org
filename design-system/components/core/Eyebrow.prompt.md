The system's connective tissue: an all-caps, wide-tracked Inter label placed directly above a headline or as a metadata run.

```jsx
<Eyebrow tone="tandoor">Wed · May 20 · 7:30 PM · Astoria</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow tone="saffron" size="sm">Next up</Eyebrow>
```

Use `tandoor` on paper, `saffron` on Ink grounds, `muted` for secondary metadata. Separate metadata segments with " · ".
