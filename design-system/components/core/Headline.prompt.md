Playfair Display headline. House style: end the sentence with a period and italicize at most one word, in an accent colour.

```jsx
<Headline level="hero">A neighborhood <em style={{fontStyle:'italic',fontWeight:500,color:'var(--jho-tandoor)'}}>chord</em>, played in Jackson Heights.</Headline>
<Headline level="h1">The mosaic palette.</Headline>
<Headline level="h2" italic>Wed · May 20 · 7:30 PM</Headline>
```

Levels: `hero` 80px, `display` 64, `h1` 56, `h2` 44 (usually italic), `h3` 30, `h4` 18.
